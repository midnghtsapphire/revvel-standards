Always-Training is the preferred name for the practice older docs call self-heal. Log three lesson types: good to-dos (patterns that worked; keep teaching them), bad to-dos (patterns that failed; do not repeat; record the vaccine), and went-raunchy (a good to-do that later turned bad; retire or revise it). The loop is Sense → Plan → Execute → Reflect → Escalate. These paths stay valid aliases: `standards/SELF_HEALING_STANDARDS.md`, `skills/self-healer`, and `scripts/agent-self-heal.js`. The paste-ready orchestrator prompt is `docs/prompts/agents/XP-SWARM-ORCHESTRATOR.md`, with the shorter companion at `docs/prompts/agents/XP-SWARM-ORCHESTRATOR-OVERVIEW.md`.
