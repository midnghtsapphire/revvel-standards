# WR-4484 — Autonomous BNAT Invention Loop (Human-Gated)

**Register:** wr-register
**Band:** 44xx
**Revision:** 0
**Status:** Draft
**Autonomy Directive:** HUMAN MERGE REQUIRED

---

## 1. Purpose

WR-4484 defines the **Autonomous BNAT (Big-New-Adjacent-Transformative) Invention Loop**: a repeatable pipeline in which software agents scan for opportunity gaps, invent candidate solutions, screen them under kill discipline, decide with an ensemble ranking, build M1 (milestone-1) skeletons, and draft intellectual-property artifacts — while a **hard human-merge gate** blocks any external launch, listing, filing, or public disclosure.

This loop is the invention engine feeding the PRIME DIRECTIVE ladder ($10k/mo → $30k/mo → $100k/mo → $10M total). It exists to produce a *steady, calibrated* stream of vetted product candidates, not to autonomously ship.

---

## 2. Scope

- **In-scope:** ideation, opportunity scoring, invention drafting, DOE-5 screening, WR-4483 ensemble decisions, M1 code/spec skeletons, provisional-IP draft text, internal PRs.
- **Out-of-scope (hard-gated):** publishing to Polar.sh, listing on marketplaces, USPTO/EPO/WIPO filings, press, customer-facing launches, contract execution, paid ads.

All out-of-scope actions require a human reviewer to (a) merge the PR and (b) affirmatively check the launch checklist in §7.

---

## 3. Hard Constraints (non-negotiable)

1. **No autonomous filing.** Agents may draft provisional patent text, trademark specimens, or copyright deposits, but only a human of record may sign and file. Grounded in *Thaler v. Vidal*, 43 F.4th 1207 (Fed. Cir. 2022) — an AI cannot be a named inventor under 35 U.S.C. §100(f) — and the U.S. Copyright Office *Statement of Policy: Works Containing Material Generated by Artificial Intelligence*, 88 Fed. Reg. 16190 (Mar. 16, 2023) — human authorship required.
2. **No autonomous public disclosure.** A public disclosure starts the 12-month U.S. patent clock (35 U.S.C. §102(b)(1)) and can destroy absolute novelty abroad. Agents post only to private repos and private issue threads.
3. **No autonomous financial commitment.** No agent may spend, list a paid product, or enter a contract.
4. **Kill discipline is a KPI.** A 0% kill rate over any 10-candidate window is a red flag and auto-pages the reviewer. See §5.3.
5. **Calibration over approval.** The optimization target is Brier score < 0.20 on agent forecasts, *not* PR-approval rate. See §8.
6. **WIP cap.** Little's Law (L = λW): with target throughput λ = 2 M1 skeletons/week and target lead time W = 2 weeks, WIP cap L ≤ 4 concurrent candidates. Enforced by the orchestrator.
7. **Every stage cites its math.** No stage may output a numeric score without naming the method (Ulwick, TRIZ, DOE-5, RICE, AHP, Brier, Little's Law). Untraceable numbers are auto-rejected.

## 4. Pipeline Stages

```
 SCAN → INVENT → SCREEN → DECIDE → BUILD → DRAFT-IP → GATE → (human) → LAUNCH
 [Ulwick] [TRIZ] [DOE-5] [WR-4483] [M1] [provisional] [HUMAN]
```

### 4.1 SCAN — Opportunity discovery

- **Input:** OSINT feeds, GitHub trending, Polar.sh funding signals, keyword-gap reports, existing WR-register.
- **Method:** **Ulwick Opportunity Score** = Importance + max(0, Importance − Satisfaction), on a 1–10 scale. Candidates with score ≥ 12 advance.
- **Output:** `scan/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` with cited sources.

### 4.2 INVENT — Candidate generation

- **Method:** **TRIZ** 40 inventive principles + adjacent-possible search over the existing WR-register. Each candidate must map to at least one TRIZ principle by number (e.g., #1 Segmentation, #15 Dynamics, #35 Parameter Change).
- **Output:** 3–7 variants per opportunity, each with a one-paragraph mechanism-of-action.

### 4.3 SCREEN — DOE-5 kill discipline

- **Method:** **DOE-5** (Design-of-Experiments, 5-factor kill screen). Each candidate is scored 0/1 on:
 1. **Novelty** — non-obvious vs. prior art hits (top-20 Google Scholar + top-20 GitHub).
 2. **Feasibility** — buildable to M1 in ≤ 2 weeks by one engineer.
 3. **Distribution** — a named channel exists (Polar, GH README, HN, existing list).
 4. **Willingness-to-pay signal** — ≥ 1 external artifact (bounty, funded repo, paid comp).
 5. **Legal headroom** — no obvious FTO (freedom-to-operate) block.
- **Rule:** any 0 → kill. Score of 5/5 required to advance.
- **Target:** kill rate ∈ [40%, 90%]. Outside that band the screen is miscalibrated.

### 4.4 DECIDE — WR-4483 ensemble ranking

- **Method:** WR-4483 ensemble of **AHP** (Analytic Hierarchy Process, Saaty 1980; consistency ratio CR < 0.10) and **RICE** (Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort, Intercom 2016).
- **Rule:** rank surviving candidates; top-k where k respects the Little's-Law WIP cap (§3.6).
- **Output:** `decide/<slug>.json` with both scores, weights, and CR.

### 4.5 BUILD — M1 skeleton

- **Deliverable:** minimal runnable artifact (CLI, script, or endpoint) + `README.md` + `TESTS.md` (even if empty stubs) + a `RISKS.md` naming the top-3 failure modes.
- **Budget:** ≤ 2 weeks wall-clock, ≤ 400 LOC net-new for the M1.

### 4.6 DRAFT-IP — Provisional artifacts

- **Agents may draft:** provisional-patent specification (35 U.S.C. §111(b)), invention disclosure form, trademark word-mark description, copyright deposit copy.
- **Agents may not:** sign, file, or pay any fee.
- **Every draft carries a banner:** `DRAFT — NOT FILED — HUMAN INVENTOR OF RECORD REQUIRED (Thaler v. Vidal, 2022; USCO 2023).`

### 4.7 GATE — Human merge (hard stop)

No downstream action occurs without a human reviewer:

- opening the PR,
- reading `RISKS.md`, `decide/<slug>.json`, and any DRAFT-IP,
- checking every box in §7,
- clicking Merge.

CI blocks merge if any §7 checkbox is unchecked. Merge triggers LAUNCH.

## 5. Metrics & KPIs

### 5.1 Throughput
- λ = M1 skeletons merged per week. **Target ≥ 2.**

### 5.2 Calibration
- **Brier score** = (1/N) Σ (p_i − o_i)² over agent forecasts of "will this candidate reach $1k MRR within 90 days." **Target < 0.20.** Weekly rolling window, N ≥ 10.

### 5.3 Kill rate
- Fraction of candidates killed at SCREEN. **Target 40%–90%.** 0% for 10 consecutive candidates auto-pages reviewer with severity=high.

### 5.4 Lead time
- Median SCAN→GATE wall-clock. **Target ≤ 14 days.**

### 5.5 Revenue contribution (PRIME DIRECTIVE)
- Sum of MRR attributable to WR-4484-originated candidates. Tracked monthly against $10k → $30k → $100k → $10M ladder.

## 7. Launch Checklist (must be 100% checked before merge)

- [ ] I have read `RISKS.md`.
- [ ] I have read `decide/<slug>.json` and I accept the AHP + RICE ranking.
- [ ] Kill rate over the last 10 candidates is within [40%, 90%].
- [ ] Brier score over the last 10 forecasts is < 0.20 (or explicitly waived with reason).
- [ ] Any DRAFT-IP is marked DRAFT and no filing has been submitted.
- [ ] No public disclosure has occurred prior to this merge.
- [ ] I am the inventor/author of record for any IP artifact carried forward.
- [ ] Little's Law WIP cap (≤ 4 concurrent) is respected post-merge.
- [ ] Revenue attribution field is filled in the tracker.

---

## 8. Optimization Target

The loop optimizes **calibration**, not approval rate. A reviewer who rubber-stamps every PR degrades Brier score and is corrected by the calibration KPI (§5.2). A reviewer who kills everything violates the kill-rate ceiling (§5.3). Both failure modes are visible in the dashboard.
