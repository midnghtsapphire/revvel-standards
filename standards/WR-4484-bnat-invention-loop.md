# WR-4484 — Autonomous BNAT Invention Loop (Human-Gated)

**Register:** wr-register
**Band:** 44xx
**Revision:** 0
**Status:** Draft
**Autonomy Directive:** HUMAN MERGE REQUIRED

---

## 1. Purpose

Define an autonomous **B**ottom-up **N**eed-**A**ttack-**T**ransform (BNAT) invention loop where agents:

1. Scan for opportunity gaps,
2. Invent candidate solutions,
3. Screen with DOE-5 kill discipline,
4. Decide via WR-4483 ensemble (AHP + RICE),
5. Build M1 skeletons,
6. Draft IP artifacts,

…with a **hard human-merge gate** before any launch, listing, filing, or public disclosure.

This standard operationalizes the PRIME DIRECTIVE ($10k/mo → $10M in 3 years) by turning invention throughput into a measurable, calibrated pipeline — while keeping legal, ethical, and quality control with humans.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all agent-driven invention activity in this repository, including:

- POLAR.SH product ideation
- OSINT tool concepts
- Automated product pipeline candidates
- Any artifact tagged `band-44xx` or `invention-loop`

Out of scope: post-merge launch execution (covered by separate WR standards) and any agent action that would constitute filing, publication, or a legal representation.

---

## 3. Hard Constraints (Non-Negotiable)

These are enforced by CI and by human review. Violation is a blocking defect.

1. **HC-1 — No autonomous filing.** Agents **draft** IP artifacts (provisional specs, claims, prior-art searches, invention disclosures). Agents **never** file with USPTO, EPO, WIPO, USCO, or any registrar.
2. **HC-2 — Human inventor/author of record.** Per *Thaler v. Vidal* (Fed. Cir. 2022) and USCO 2023 guidance ("Copyright Registration Guidance: Works Containing Material Generated by Artificial Intelligence", 88 FR 16190), only natural persons are named as inventor/author. Agents are tools, not inventors.
3. **HC-3 — Human merge gate.** No launch, listing, marketing, public disclosure, or filing occurs without an explicit human merge of a PR referencing the specific artifact.
4. **HC-4 — DOE-5 kill discipline.** Every batch of ≥5 candidates SHALL kill ≥1 candidate at screening. **0% kill rate is a red flag** and triggers WR-4484-AUDIT.
5. **HC-5 — Calibration over approval.** The pipeline KPI is **Brier score < 0.20** on agent forecasts, not approval rate. High approval + poor calibration = failure.
6. **HC-6 — Little's Law WIP cap.** Concurrent candidates in the loop are capped: `WIP ≤ throughput_target × cycle_time_target`. Overflow is queued, not force-fed.
7. **HC-7 — Named math only.** Every scoring or decision step SHALL cite a named method (see §5). No opaque "vibes" scores.
8. **HC-8 — No PII/PHI/export-controlled data** enters agent prompts without a documented DPA and jurisdictional review.

## 4. Pipeline Stages

```
[SCAN] → [INVENT] → [SCREEN] → [DECIDE] → [BUILD-M1] → [DRAFT-IP] → [GATE] → (human merge) → launch
```

### 4.1 SCAN — Opportunity discovery

- **Inputs:** market signals, GitHub issues, POLAR.SH funding trends, OSINT feeds, user interviews.
- **Method:** **Ulwick Opportunity Score** = Importance + max(0, Importance − Satisfaction), scored 1–10 per outcome statement.
- **Output:** ranked list of unmet outcomes with Opportunity Score ≥ 12.
- **Agent role:** autonomous.
- **Artifact:** `scan/YYYY-MM-DD-opportunities.md`.

### 4.2 INVENT — Candidate generation

- **Method:** **TRIZ** 40 inventive principles applied to top-N opportunities; combinatorial expansion with contradiction matrix.
- **Output:** ≥5 distinct candidate concepts per opportunity (DOE-5 minimum).
- **Agent role:** autonomous.
- **Artifact:** `invent/<opp-id>/candidates.md`.

### 4.3 SCREEN — Kill discipline

- **Method:** **DOE-5** (Design of Experiments, 5-candidate minimum) with prespecified kill criteria:
  - Technical infeasibility
  - Legal/regulatory blocker
  - Unit economics (LTV/CAC < 3)
  - Market size (TAM < $1M/yr addressable)
  - Ethical/safety veto
- **Requirement:** ≥1 kill per batch. **Kill rate is a first-class KPI.**
- **Agent role:** autonomous, but kill decisions logged with rationale.
- **Artifact:** `screen/<opp-id>/doe5-report.md`.

### 4.4 DECIDE — Ensemble ranking (WR-4483)

- **Method:** WR-4483 ensemble = **AHP** (Analytic Hierarchy Process, Saaty 1980) pairwise weights + **RICE** score (Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort).
- **Calibration:** Confidence values SHALL be tracked against outcomes via **Brier score**; target < 0.20.
- **Output:** ranked candidates with explicit weights and confidence intervals.
- **Agent role:** autonomous.
- **Artifact:** `decide/<opp-id>/wr4483-ranking.md`.

### 4.5 BUILD-M1 — Milestone-1 skeleton

- **Definition:** M1 = minimum runnable skeleton (repo scaffold, `README`, one end-to-end happy path, no production dependencies).
- **WIP cap:** enforced by **Little's Law**: `L = λW`.
- **Agent role:** autonomous.
- **Artifact:** `build/<candidate-id>/` directory.

### 4.6 DRAFT-IP — Intellectual property artifacts

- **Outputs (draft only):**
  - Prior-art search log (Google Patents, Lens.org, arXiv)
  - Invention disclosure form (IDF)
  - Provisional specification draft
  - Claim scaffolds (independent + dependent)
- **Agent role:** autonomous **drafting**; human is inventor.
- **Artifact:** `ip/<candidate-id>/` — marked `DRAFT — NOT FILED`.

### 4.7 GATE — Human merge gate ⚠️

**This is the hard stop.**

A human reviewer SHALL:

1. Verify HC-1 through HC-8 compliance.
2. Confirm inventor/author attribution is a natural person.
3. Confirm kill discipline was exercised (§4.3).
4. Confirm calibration data was logged (§4.4).
5. Merge the PR **only if** all above are satisfied.

No launch, listing, filing, or public disclosure occurs before merge. CI SHALL block merges that lack a `GATE-APPROVED-BY: <human>` trailer.

## 6. KPIs

| KPI                          | Target        | Red flag                |
|------------------------------|---------------|-------------------------|
| Brier score (agent forecasts)| < 0.20        | > 0.30                  |
| Kill rate per DOE-5 batch    | ≥ 20%         | 0% (triggers audit)     |
| Cycle time SCAN→GATE         | ≤ 14 days     | > 30 days               |
| WIP (concurrent candidates)  | ≤ Little cap  | > 1.5× cap              |
| Human gate rejection rate    | 10–40%        | < 5% or > 60%           |
| Post-launch revenue attach   | Trend upward  | Flat 3 quarters         |

**Note:** Approval rate is *not* a KPI. Calibration is.

## 7. Legal Reality Encoded

- **Inventorship (US patent):** *Thaler v. Vidal*, 43 F.4th 1207 (Fed. Cir. 2022) — inventors must be natural persons. Agents draft; humans invent.
- **Copyright:** USCO, "Copyright Registration Guidance: Works Containing Material Generated by Artificial Intelligence," 88 FR 16190 (March 16, 2023) — human authorship required for registration.
- **Filing:** Agents SHALL NOT transmit to USPTO/EPO/WIPO/USCO. All filings go through named human counsel or pro-se human filer.
- **Disclosure:** Agents SHALL NOT publish, tweet, list on marketplaces, or otherwise publicly disclose pre-gate artifacts (statutory-bar risk under 35 U.S.C. §102).

---

## 8. Audit — WR-4484-AUDIT

Triggered when:

- Kill rate hits 0% for any DOE-5 batch (HC-4).
- Brier score exceeds 0.30 over trailing 20 forecasts (HC-5).
- Any HC-1/HC-2/HC-3 violation is detected or alleged.

Audit output: root-cause memo + calibration recalibration + optional pipeline pause.
