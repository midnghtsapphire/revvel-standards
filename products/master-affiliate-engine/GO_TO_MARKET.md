**SEO Keywords:** affiliate link engine, automated affiliate marketing, multi-agent affiliate pipeline, CRM affiliate integration, TikTok affiliate automation, MCP workflows affiliate, autonomous agent pipelines

## 30-Day Launch Plan

**Week 1 — Deploy & Capture**
- Deploy to Vercel (`master-affiliate-engine.vercel.app`)
- Create TikTok green-screen video using dashboard as background
- Hook: "Stop spending months coding your stack — deploy my exact pipeline in one click"
- Post to TikTok, X, LinkedIn with link in bio pointing to `/checkout`

**Week 2 — Content Engine**
- Publish 2 use-case videos: "How I earn $2k/mo passive with 5 affiliate links"
- Offer free downloadable Blueprint PDF (lead magnet → waitlist CRM capture)
- Cross-post to IndieHackers, Reddit r/affiliatemarketing, Discord communities

**Week 3 — First 25 Paying Users**
- Run 72-hour "founding member" discount ($19/mo Launch tier)
- Email waitlist with exclusive launch pricing
- Collect 5 testimonials for social proof

**Week 4 — Agency Push**
- Pitch 3 digital agencies on white-label Agency tier ($299/mo)
- Publish case study landing page with click/commission data
- Activate automated CRM follow-up sequences
