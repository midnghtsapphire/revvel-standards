# Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) - Cradle-to-Grave Analysis

## Overview
LCA evaluates environmental impacts throughout a product's entire life cycle: from raw material extraction (cradle) through production, use, and disposal (grave).

## LCA Phases

### 1. Raw Material Extraction (Cradle)
- Mining and resource extraction
- Agricultural inputs (if applicable)
- Resource depletion rates
- Ecosystem disruption

### 2. Manufacturing & Production
- Energy consumption
- Water usage
- Emissions (air, water, waste)
- Chemical inputs
- Worker health impacts

### 3. Distribution & Logistics
- Transportation emissions
- Packaging waste
- Storage energy
- Cold chain requirements

### 4. Use Phase
- Energy consumption
- Maintenance requirements
- Consumables needed
- Repair/replacement frequency
- Operational emissions

### 5. End of Life
- Recyclability
- Compostability
- Hazardous waste concerns
- Landfill contribution
- Decommissioning energy

## Impact Categories

| Category | What It Measures | Unit |
|----------|------------------|------|
| Global Warming | Greenhouse gas emissions | kg CO2e |
| Ozone Depletion | CFCs, HCFCs | kg CFC-11e |
| Acidification | SOx, NOx emissions | kg SO2e |
| Eutrophication | Nutrient runoff | kg PO4e |
| Smog Formation | VOCs, NOx | kg O3e |
| Resource Depletion | Minerals, fossil fuels | kg Sbe |
| Water Use | Freshwater consumption | L |
| Land Use | Land transformation | m2 |
