The Bench

Every naive red-light build dies here. Dose is not "more light." Response is biphasic — the Arndt–Schulz inverted U. Below threshold, nothing. Above it, inhibition. Set your numbers and find out which build you actually have.

Schematic Arndt–Schulz form. Not a predictive model — deliberately. Anchors: fluence commonly 1–20 J/cm²; irradiance commonly 5–50 mW/cm² for stimulation and healing; 1–5 J/cm² reported ideal for proliferation; peak ATP at ~3 J/cm² at 25 mW/cm² in cortical neurons. Distance drives panel falloff, not the fluence math — set irradiance to what your meter actually reads at that distance.

Red enters the cell — the mitochondria stir. Too much, and they sleep.

Why irradiance is not a proxy for dose

Same joules, different power, different outcome. In a hamster oral mucositis model, 660nm delivering an identical 0.9 J/cm² per point reduced clinical severity at 55 mW/cm² and did nothing measurable at 155 mW/cm². The fleet therefore treats irradiance and fluence as two independent gated parameters. Neither one alone is a dose. This single result is why the Bench has two sliders where most calculators have one.

Exploit this asymmetry — it may be the most valuable thing in the thread. Adverse events are mandatory reportable. Null results are not publishable. Searching for damage is a higher-yield retrieval strategy than searching for benefit — the exact opposite of what everyone does. This generalizes far beyond light.

KILL-07 · 15Hz is the worst number you could have picked two harms, stacked

You said "15Hz shirt" from your first message. 15Hz is not inert.

Harm 1 — seizure. Photosensitivity is an abnormal EEG response to light or pattern stimulation, present in roughly 0.3–3% of people. Frequencies of 15–25 Hz are the most provocative, though the range runs 1–65 Hz. In the Pokémon broadcast incident, 685 children went to hospital after red–blue flashes — and only 24% of those who seized had ever seized before. Red is a listed factor. Your device is red.

Harm 2 — flicker vertigo, and this one has no screening criterion. The Bucha effect: disorientation, vertigo and nausea from a strobe flashing at 1–20 Hz, corresponding roughly to human brainwave frequencies. The Avedro patent states it plainly — the effects resemble photosensitive seizures but are not restricted to people with histories of epilepsy.

Seizure is rare (~1 in 4,000) and screenable. Vertigo is neither. 15Hz sits inside both bands at once.

THE FIX, and it was hiding in a corneal surgery patent disclosed, not claimed

Randomized asynchronous pulsed topographic patterning — non-periodic, uniformly appearing illumination that eliminates the possibility of triggering photosensitive seizures or flicker vertigo for pulse frequencies between 2 Hz and 84 Hz.

Randomize the phase and there is no periodic drive to entrain to. You keep the photochemistry of pulsing and lose the neurology. It sits in the specification, not the claims — disclosed art. US 9,707,126 B2 — Avedro

THE OTHER FIX — found by the Watchtower on its first run skip the eyes entirely

The seizure and vertigo pathways are both visual. So: don't use the eyes. A 2026-07-10 paper compares vibrotactile stimulation to combined visual+auditory for gamma entrainment. Vibration at 40Hz has no photosensitive pathway at all — the entire hazard class evaporates. PMID 42426112

It does not have a Hz. Rayon is regenerated cellulose; optically unremarkable. There is no measurement that returns "15Hz" from a shirt, which means there is nothing to overlay, tune, or improve. Nearest real thing: the KAIST textile NIR OLED in Shard 05 — it works because it is a device shaped like fabric, not fabric that is a device.
