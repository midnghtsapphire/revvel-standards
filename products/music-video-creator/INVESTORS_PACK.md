# INVESTORS PACK: Music Video Creator

## Executive Summary
Music Video Creator automatically bridges the gap between raw audio and visual engagement by automatically animating avatars and syncing them to .wav files. Targeted at independent musicians, influencers, and podcasters.

## Market Opportunity
The demand for low-friction video content is at an all-time high. Creators need to push their audio tracks to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram Reels but lack the budget for full-scale video production.

## Revenue Model
- **Freemium**: 1 free watermarked generation per week.
- **Premium**: Subscription based token system for high-definition, un-watermarked videos.
- **Affiliate Marketing**: Embedded links to audio/video tools and hosting platforms.
