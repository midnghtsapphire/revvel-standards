# Go-to-Market — OpenMythos

## Positioning

**OpenMythos** is a glassmorphic template-driven launch engine for creators and studios who want to turn worldbuilding concepts into sellable digital offers.

## Ideal Customer

- Indie worldbuilders and narrative creators
- Small game/story studios shipping new IP
- Creative agencies packaging lore-driven campaigns

## Revenue Model

| Offer | Price | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $19 one-time | Template pack + launch page |
| Pro | $79/month | Myth workspace + automation prompts |
| Studio | $399 sprint | Done-with-you ship-to-market setup |

## Launch Plan

1. **Week 1:** Launch waitlist page + announce in creator communities.
2. **Week 2:** Publish 2 showcase templates and 1 case-study landing page.
3. **Week 3:** Sell first 25 Starter packs with time-boxed pricing.
4. **Week 4:** Convert waitlist to Pro beta and collect testimonials.

## Primary Acquisition Channels

- SEO content for worldbuilding + launch templates
- Creator communities (Reddit, Discord, X)
- Partner cross-promos with writing/game dev tool creators

## Core Metrics

- Waitlist conversion rate
- Starter pack sales volume
- Pro trial-to-paid conversion
- Monthly recurring revenue
