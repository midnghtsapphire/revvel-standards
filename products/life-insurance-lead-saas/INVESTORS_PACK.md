# INVESTORS_PACK - Life Wizard Insurance Lead SaaS

## The Problem
Insurance agents need high-intent, qualified leads. Current solutions offer shared, low-quality, or fake leads that waste agent time and budget.

## The Solution
Life Wizard provides pre-qualified live transfers and exclusive outbound leads, verified and pre-screened.

## The Market
Life and Health Insurance agents, independent brokers, and agencies.

## Business Model
- Live Transfers: $38-$40 per transfer
- Exclusive Outbound: $14-$16 per lead
- Aged Leads: $1-$5
- Secondary Revenue: Affiliate commissions on CRM and hosting tools

## Go-To-Market
- Direct outreach to insurance agencies
- Content marketing (SEO, YouTube)
- Paid ads (Meta, Google)
