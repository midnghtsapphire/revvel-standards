## Load-bearing (safe to act on)

- **[PROVEN]** UV-C (~254 nm) inactivates surface pathogens; well-characterized germicidal dosimetry.
- **[PROVEN]** Blue light (~415–450 nm) has photochemical effects on *Cutibacterium acnes* (porphyrin photoexcitation).
- **[PROVEN]** Red (~630–660 nm) and NIR (~810–850 nm) penetrate tissue at characterized depths; used in FDA-cleared PBM devices.
- **[PROVEN]** Ocular hazard thresholds (ICNIRP, IEC 62471) are well-defined for the relevant bands.

## Aspirational (interesting, needs work)

- **[EMERGING]** 405 nm violet for continuous low-level environmental decontamination — evidence exists but dose/duration windows are still being characterized.
- **[EMERGING]** NIR PBM for mitochondrial cytochrome-c-oxidase modulation — mechanism plausible, clinical endpoints heterogeneous.
- **[EMERGING]** Circadian entrainment via targeted blue-enriched morning light — supported for some populations, dose-response variable.

## Speculative (kept for reading, DO NOT SHIP)

- **[SPECULATIVE]** Frequency-specific "resonance" claims tying named wavelengths to named organs/diseases without dosimetry.
- **[SPECULATIVE]** Sub-milliwatt "information-carrying light" pathways.
- **[SPECULATIVE]** Chromotherapy color-emotion mappings presented as physiological.

## How to read this

Every substantive claim below carries an evidence tag placed in front of the bullet. The tags reflect *my* assessment of the claim's evidentiary standing, not the deck's own confidence (the deck presents nearly everything as settled fact).

- **[PROVEN]** — supported by human RCTs, systematic reviews, a standards body, or a datasheet-grade spec.
- **[EMERGING]** — in vitro, animal model, preprint, or a single small human trial (n < 50).
- **[SPECULATIVE]** — mechanistic reasoning, analogy, marketing framing, or aging-reversal narrative not established by controlled human evidence.
- *Untagged* — descriptive scaffolding, not a testable claim; not assessed.

Caveat: the source is an infographic, not a paper. It names no specific studies by title, gives no citations, and reports effect sizes without protocols. Several figures (the "20% glucose" and "20% color contrast" numbers) trace to real Jeffery-lab work, but I am tagging them from the deck's own framing, not from a retrieved primary source. ## Section-by-section summary

### 1. Wavelength → cellular impact table
- Untagged: Deck buckets light into 400–500nm (cyan/blue), 550–600nm (gold/green), and 600–850nm (red/NIR).
- [PROVEN] Short-wavelength (blue) light affects circadian rhythm and sleep-wake regulation via photoreceptors. (Well established in human chronobiology.)
- [EMERGING] Red/NIR (600–850nm) "penetrates tissue, enhances mitochondrial function (ATP production), reduces inflammation, promotes repair." (Directionally supported by photobiomodulation literature; effect sizes and clinical endpoints remain mostly animal/small-trial.)
- [EMERGING] Clinical applications listed for red/NIR: tissue regeneration, wound healing, age-related macular degeneration (AMD), neuroprotection.

### 2. The Invisible Public Health Crisis
- [SPECULATIVE] "This is an issue on the same level as asbestos." — Dr. Glen Jeffery. (Quoted analogy / risk framing, not a quantified risk comparison.)
- [SPECULATIVE] Modern white LEDs are "stripped of therapeutic red wavelengths and heavily enriched with short-wavelength blue light" framed as a population-scale harm. (The spectral characterization is partly true — see below — but the "public health crisis" framing is advocacy.)
- [PROVEN] White LEDs do emit a blue-heavy spectrum with little red/NIR relative to sunlight or incandescent. (This is a straightforward spectral-power fact, verifiable from lamp datasheets.)
- [EMERGING] "Prolonged exposure induces cellular stress, raises blood glucose, and degrades mitochondrial function in real-time." (Jeffery-lab claim; small-scale human/animal signal, not established as a general population effect.)

### 3. Light is Biological Nutrition (Nutrition Facts panel)
- Untagged framing device: light presented as a "nutrient."
- [PROVEN] Short wavelengths (UV/blue) signal morning wakefulness and drive vitamin D synthesis (UVB); in excess UV causes cellular/DNA damage.
- Untagged: Medium wavelengths (green/yellow) support visual acuity and contrast.
- [SPECULATIVE] Long wavelengths (red/NIR) are "the missing nutrient in modern indoor life" providing "deep cellular healing." (Narrative framing of the therapeutic claim.)

### 4. Navigating the Frequencies (spectral map)
- [PROVEN] UV (300–400nm) is largely blocked by cornea and skin surface.
- [SPECULATIVE→EMERGING] "420nm–440nm (The Danger Zone): the exact frequency range that suppresses mitochondrial breathing." (Precise band attribution stated as fact; the specific "suppresses mitochondrial breathing" mechanism at that band is a lab hypothesis, not settled.)
- [PROVEN] Visible red stops ~700nm; beyond that is invisible NIR. (Optics fact.)
- [EMERGING] "The Healing Zone" 650–900nm labeled as therapeutic red/NIR.

### 5. Spiky vs. Smooth Energy Delivery
- [SPECULATIVE] Sun and incandescent fire deliver a "smooth" broadband spectrum that "allows mitochondria to process energy efficiently without stress," whereas LED's "compressed, jagged delivery" is registered as "unnatural stress leading to cellular exhaustion." (The spectral-shape difference is real; the claim that spectral *smoothness itself* is what mitochondria sense as stress is mechanistic speculation.)

### 6. The Ocean Analogy: Why Cells Absorb Red Light
- [PROVEN] In water, blue light scatters/reflects while red is absorbed within the first few feet. (Established water optics.)
- [SPECULATIVE] Extending this: cells are mostly water, so "blue light bounces off structures but red light is absorbed directly by the viscous 'nano-water' inside the cell, storing the energy." (Analogy; the intracellular-water absorption-and-storage mechanism is a hypothesis.)

### 7. The Penetration Blueprint
- [PROVEN] UV/short blue bounces off epidermis/cornea and causes surface inflammation.
- [EMERGING] 670–900nm passes through skin, "ignores solid bone, and scatters deep into the cerebral cortex." (Deep NIR tissue penetration is real and measurable; the claim of meaningful *cerebral cortex* dosing transcranially in humans is contested and dose-limited.)
- [SPECULATIVE] "Standard White/Blue LED stops entirely at the soft tissue layer. Does not penetrate bone." (Over-crisp binary.)

### 8. The Viscosity Motor
- [SPECULATIVE] Intracellular "nano-water" is viscous, forcing the mitochondrial ATP pump to work harder; red light is absorbed by the water, thins its viscosity, and lets "the motor spin freely, instantly ramping up ATP production." (This is the deck's central mechanistic story — the Gerald Pollack "fourth-phase / structured water" style hypothesis applied to mitochondria. Not established biophysics.)

### 9. Laying Down More Tracks (structural adaptation)
- [SPECULATIVE] Immediate effect: the "viscosity drop" removes friction so electrons (the "energy train") move faster along the transport chain.
- [EMERGING→SPECULATIVE] Structural upgrade: the cell "detects the increased speed" and "physically synthesizes more electron-passing proteins," permanently raising capacity. (Upregulation of respiratory-chain components after light exposure is plausible and partly seen in models; the causal chain as narrated is speculative.)

### 10. Canceling the 'Eat Me' Signal
- [EMERGING] Energy-depleted mitochondria trigger apoptosis by presenting a molecular "eat me" signal, driving aging and neurodegeneration.
- [SPECULATIVE] Red light artificially boosts ATP, raising the "battery charge" above a critical threshold, canceling the "eat me" signal so the cell survives. (Simplified rescue narrative.)

### 11. The Ripple Effect: The Mitochondrial Community
- [EMERGING] Mitochondria communicate; local red-light stimulation causes cells to secrete microvesicles and protective cytokines into the bloodstream.
- [SPECULATIVE] These messengers travel systemically and share "protective energy upgrades" with distant unexposed organs including the brain. (Systemic/abscopal photobiomodulation is an active but early hypothesis.)

### 12. Systemic Communication Maps (the two headline metrics)
- [EMERGING] Blood Glucose Study: red light "reduced the dangerous blood glucose spike from a sugar tolerance test by over 20%." (Attributable to a small Jeffery-lab human study; single small trial.)
- [EMERGING] Parkinson's Model: red light "drastically reduced the magnitude of neural cell death, offsetting degeneration" (substantia nigra target). (Animal/model-level; not human clinical outcome.)

### 13. Protocol 1: Reversing Retinal Aging
- [EMERGING] The retina has the body's highest mitochondrial concentration ("the sports car of organs — burns out fast").
- [EMERGING] Protocol: 670nm deep red, 3 minutes exposure, once every 5 days ("the cellular upgrade lasts precisely 5 days"), yielding ~20% improvement in color contrast thresholds in older individuals. (Maps to a published small Jeffery-lab study; the "precisely 5 days" durability is stated more firmly than a small trial supports.)

### 14. The Circadian Window
- [EMERGING] Morning (pre-11:00 AM) is the "Production Phase" when mitochondria make peak ATP; red light in this window "highly optimizes function."
- [SPECULATIVE] Afternoon (post-11:00 AM) is the "Maintenance Phase" (cellular "ironing," ER junctioning) and red light then "yields minimal to no clinical effect." (Time-of-day dependence is an interesting emerging idea; the hard 11:00 AM cutoff is over-specified.)

### 15. The Light Source Diagnostic Matrix
- [PROVEN] Spectral characterization across sources: sunlight and halogen/incandescent are broad & smooth with high/massive infrared; modern LEDs are spiky & compressed with zero-to-low IR; clinical red panels are targeted & narrow. (Spectral facts.)
- [EMERGING/SPECULATIVE] The "cellular impact" column (LEDs "induce mitochondrial decline," halogen "highly protective") — impact labels are the deck's interpretation, not spec.

### 16. The Danger of Lasers (Caustics)
- [PROVEN] Lasers are monochromatic and coherent; focusing/caustic effects can concentrate energy and damage retinal tissue. Do not use monochromatic laser devices for photobiomodulation without a professional; prefer LED panels. (Sound safety guidance; laser eye hazard is well established.)

### 17. The Protocol Parameter Matrix
- [PROVEN] Dose for photobiomodulation should be measured in mW/cm² (irradiance), not lux. Lux measures perceived brightness and is not a valid dosimetry unit for this. (Correct — this is the right metric.)
- [EMERGING] Target 650–900nm; wavelengths below 650nm "lose clinical efficacy." (Roughly consistent with the field's optical window, though ~630nm is also used.)
- [EMERGING] Effective irradiance 1–8 mW/cm²; early studies used ~40 mW/cm²; "dim light is highly effective." (Biphasic dose-response / low-dose efficacy is a real theme in the literature.)
- [EMERGING] Long wavelengths "easily penetrate up to 6 layers of clothing"; skin exposure optimal but light clothing is not a barrier.

### 18. Architectural Biology Hacks
- [SPECULATIVE] Keep a dimmable halogen/incandescent lamp on for smooth infrared even when dim. (Reasonable if IR exposure helps, but the benefit at ambient dim levels is unestablished.)
- [SPECULATIVE] Place green leafy plants near the desk because "plant matter reflects ambient infrared back into your environment." (The reflectance claim is thin; dubious as a meaningful dosing method.)
- [PROVEN/EMERGING] Avoid heavy infrared-blocking (low-E tinted) window glass that filters out daylight IR. (Low-E glass does attenuate IR — that's a verifiable glazing fact; whether it matters biologically is the emerging part.)

### 19. Restoring the Balance (closing thesis)
- [SPECULATIVE] We evolved under smooth broadband sunlight, not jagged blue screens or laser beams; restoring long-wavelength exposure heals "brain, eyes, and metabolic system" without a drug — "give our cellular water the light it was designed to absorb." (Evolutionary-mismatch narrative; the deck's overall thesis statement.)

## Load-bearing vs aspirational

**Load-bearing — if true, these would actually change a build/product decision (OSINT/product-pipeline angle: a red-light hardware or protocol product):**
- [PROVEN] Dose is mW/cm², not lux. Any product spec, marketing claim, or measurement tooling must use irradiance. Non-negotiable and cheap to get right.
- [EMERGING] Effective window 650–900nm; low irradiance (1–8 mW/cm²) works. Drives LED bill-of-materials, panel power budget, and safety margin — you don't need a high-power (and more dangerous) device.
- [PROVEN] Use LED panels, not lasers; caustic/coherence hazard is real. Product-safety and liability decision.
- [EMERGING] The 670nm / 3-min / 5-day retinal protocol and the ~20% glucose and ~20% color-contrast numbers are the concrete efficacy hooks. If a claim is made in copy, it must be sourced to the actual small trials, and labeled as small-n — these are the marketing-vs-legal boundary.
- [PROVEN] LED spectra are blue-heavy with little red/NIR. Verifiable, safe to state, and the honest core of the "modern indoor light lacks red" pitch.

**Aspirational / narrative — do NOT hang a build or a claim on these:**
- The entire "viscosity motor / nano-water / fourth-phase water" mechanism (structured-water viscosity as a molecular motor). Compelling story, unproven biophysics.
- "Same level as asbestos" and the "invisible public health crisis" framing. Advocacy, legally risky if repeated as fact.
- Systemic "mitochondrial community" upgrades reaching the brain from local exposure.
- The hard 11:00 AM circadian cutoff and "upgrade lasts precisely 5 days" precision.
- "Reverses aging," "reverses retinal aging," neuroprotection-as-cure framing.
- The plant-as-IR-reflector and halogen-dimmer "hacks."

## BLANK — what this source does not establish

- **No citations.** Names no study titles, journals, authors beyond "Jeffery" and "Huberman Lab," no DOIs, no dates. Every number is uncited.
- **No sample sizes, protocols, or controls** for the glucose or color-contrast results — cannot assess n, blinding, or replication from this source.
- **No dose-response curve or safety thresholds** beyond a stated 1–8 mW/cm² range; no exposure-duration limits, no contraindications, no eye-safety irradiance ceiling.
- **No mechanism validation** — the viscosity/nano-water motor is asserted, never demonstrated.
- **No population evidence** for the "LED public health crisis"; no epidemiology, no comparison cohorts.
- **No device specifications** — no specific product, panel geometry, distance, or duty cycle that would let you reproduce a protocol.
- **No adverse-event or null-result reporting** — the deck is entirely one-directional; no failed trials or limits are shown.
- **Nothing distinguishes correlation from causation** in the human observations.
- **It is an infographic, not primary literature** — it cannot serve as a citation itself; treat it as a map of hypotheses to verify, not evidence.
