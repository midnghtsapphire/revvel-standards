## Load-bearing (safe to act on)

- **[PROVEN]** UV-C (~254 nm) inactivates surface pathogens; well-characterized germicidal dosimetry.
- **[PROVEN]** Blue light (~415–450 nm) has photochemical effects on *Cutibacterium acnes* (porphyrin photoexcitation).
- **[PROVEN]** Red (~630–660 nm) and NIR (~810–850 nm) penetrate tissue at characterized depths; used in FDA-cleared PBM devices.
- **[PROVEN]** Ocular hazard thresholds (ICNIRP, IEC 62471) are well-defined for the relevant bands.

## Aspirational (interesting, needs work)

- **[EMERGING]** 405 nm violet for continuous low-level environmental decontamination — evidence exists but dose/duration windows are still being characterized.
- **[EMERGING]** NIR PBM for mitochondrial cytochrome-c-oxidase modulation — mechanism plausible, clinical endpoints heterogeneous.
- **[EMERGING]** Circadian entrainment via targeted blue-enriched morning light — supported for some populations, dose-response variable.

## Speculative (kept for reading, DO NOT SHIP)

- **[SPECULATIVE]** Frequency-specific "resonance" claims tying named wavelengths to named organs/diseases without dosimetry.
- **[SPECULATIVE]** Sub-milliwatt "information-carrying light" pathways.
- **[SPECULATIVE]** Chromotherapy color-emotion mappings presented as physiological.
