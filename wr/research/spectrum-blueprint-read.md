# Spectrum Blueprint — Read-Through

**Source:** uncited infographic (Drive). Tags below assess *claim-standing*,
not literature verification. No citations are fabricated.

Legend: `[PROVEN]` reproducible in peer-reviewed literature ·
`[EMERGING]` early signal, limited replication ·
`[SPECULATIVE]` mechanism-of-action guess or marketing claim.

## Load-bearing (keep, act on)
- `[PROVEN]` 660 nm and 830–850 nm penetrate skin and modulate cytochrome c oxidase activity in vitro.
- `[PROVEN]` Dose-response is biphasic (Arndt-Schulz); more is not better.
- `[PROVEN]` Ocular exposure limits (ICNIRP) are non-negotiable for any device operating in the visible/NIR band.
- `[EMERGING]` Pulsed vs continuous wave shows tissue-dependent differences; not yet a settled parameter.
- `[EMERGING]` 1064 nm shows deeper penetration but requires higher fluence for equivalent photon delivery.

## Aspirational (keep for research, do not ship as claims)
- `[SPECULATIVE]` Systemic effects from localized irradiation via circulating factors.
- `[SPECULATIVE]` Mitochondrial "reset" framing — mechanism plausible, endpoint underspecified.
- `[SPECULATIVE]` Cognitive/mood endpoints from transcranial NIR — signal exists, effect sizes contested.
- `[SPECULATIVE]` Specific-frequency "resonance" claims (e.g. 40 Hz gamma entrainment coupling to photobiomodulation).

## BLANK
_Reserved for claims we cannot yet tag either way. Add here rather than
force a category._

## Reading discipline
Speculative material is retained, not deleted. It is *labeled*. A reader
should be able to distinguish what the field has shown from what a
vendor infographic asserts. When in doubt: BLANK, not SPECULATIVE, not PROVEN.
