## Load-bearing (drives dose-engine defaults)

- [PROVEN] 660 nm and 850 nm are the two most-studied photobiomodulation (PBM) wavelengths; both have peer-reviewed dose-response data.
- [PROVEN] Irradiance × time = fluence (J/cm²); biphasic dose-response (Arndt–Schulz) is repeatedly observed in PBM literature.
- [PROVEN] Ocular exposure limits exist (ICNIRP); near-IR retinal thermal limit is the binding constraint at 850 nm for face-adjacent use.
- [EMERGING] 810 nm transcranial for cognitive endpoints — multiple small RCTs, heterogeneous protocols; effect sizes not yet stable.
- [EMERGING] 1064 nm for deeper tissue penetration — mechanistic plausibility, thin clinical corpus.

- [SPECULATIVE] Coherence / polarization effects at PBM intensities producing clinically distinct outcomes vs. incoherent LEDs.
