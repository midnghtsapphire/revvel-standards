## Load-Bearing (used by the bench)

### [PROVEN] Photobiomodulation dose windows
Red (630–670 nm) and near-IR (810–850 nm) have replicated dose-response curves in peer-reviewed literature for wound healing and mitochondrial ATP output at fluences roughly 1–10 J/cm². Biphasic response (too much = null or negative) is well established.

### [PROVEN] Ocular safety thresholds
ICNIRP and ANSI Z136 limits for coherent and incoherent optical radiation are the operational ceiling. The bench's OCULAR gate is anchored here.

### [EMERGING] 40 Hz gamma entrainment
Flicker at 40 Hz shows promising results in murine models and small human trials for amyloid clearance markers. Human clinical endpoints remain unconfirmed at scale. Bench treats 40 Hz as a study variable, not a therapy claim.

### [EMERGING] Transcranial NIR
Skull-penetration modeling and pilot trials for depression / TBI show signal. Effect sizes vary; blinding is hard. Bench logs but does not recommend.
