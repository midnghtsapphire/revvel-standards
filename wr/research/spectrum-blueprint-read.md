# Spectrum Blueprint — Read-Through

**Source:** uncited infographic. Tags below are an *assessment of claim-standing*, not literature verification. **No citations are fabricated.**

Speculative material is retained (not deleted) for reading and research context.

---

## Load-Bearing (governs product decisions)

- **[PROVEN]** Visible light spans ~380–780 nm; photopic peak ~555 nm.
- **[PROVEN]** Melanopic (ipRGC) sensitivity peaks near 480 nm and drives circadian entrainment.
- **[PROVEN]** Blue-light hazard weighting (ICNIRP) peaks ~435–440 nm for acute retinal photochemical injury.
- **[EMERGING]** Narrow-band 670 nm photobiomodulation shows mitochondrial cytochrome-c-oxidase effects in bench and small clinical studies.
- **[EMERGING]** Flicker below ~90 Hz PWM is detectable by a nontrivial fraction of sensitive users; IEEE 1789 provides low-risk envelopes.

## Aspirational (kept for research, not shipped as claims)

- **[SPECULATIVE]** Specific nm bands "detox" or "reset" endocrine axes on the timescales the infographic implies.
- **[SPECULATIVE]** Whole-body single-session dose equivalences across skin, retina, and CNS.
- **[SPECULATIVE]** Claimed synergies between UV-A microdoses and NIR at fixed ratios.
- **[SPECULATIVE]** Population-level dose–response curves inferred from n<30 studies.

## BLANK

Items the infographic asserts but for which we have neither a proven nor an emerging basis to tag. Left blank on purpose — filling them in would require sourcing we do not have.

- Band-specific "organ resonance" claims.
- Numeric "optimal minutes/day" per band without irradiance qualification.
- Cross-band additivity claims stated without a photobiological model.

---

## Reading Notes

1. The infographic conflates *irradiance* with *dose*; any product spec must state W/m² **and** exposure duration.
2. Melanopic lux ≠ photopic lux — separate reporting is required for circadian claims.
3. Blue-light hazard and melanopic action spectra overlap but are **not** the same curve; conflating them is a common failure mode.
4. NIR penetration depth depends strongly on tissue and wavelength; "deep" is not a spec.

## Do-Not-Ship List

Until sourced, do not ship any customer-facing copy that:
- Assigns minutes/day dosages without irradiance.
- Claims organ-specific resonance.
- Uses "detox," "reset," or "heal" as verbs tied to a band.
