## Load-bearing (informs WR-4600 Photon Bench dose engine)

### Red / near-infrared PBM (620–680 nm, 800–880 nm)
- `[PROVEN]` Cytochrome-c-oxidase absorption peaks in the ~620–680 nm and
  ~800–880 nm bands are the standard mechanistic rationale for PBM dosing.
- `[PROVEN]` Fluence windows on the order of 1–20 J/cm² are the range most
  commonly cited in clinical PBM literature; below and above this range,
  bidirectional (hormetic) response is widely reported.
- `[EMERGING]` Pulsed delivery (typ. 10–40 Hz) yielding different outcomes
  than CW at matched average power. Multiple small RCTs; not settled.
- `[EMERGING]` Transcranial 810 nm for cognition / mood: signal exists,
  effect size heterogeneous.

### Ocular / flicker safety (gates the dashboard)
- `[PROVEN]` ICNIRP/ANSI Z136 limits for retinal exposure — hard gate.
- `[PROVEN]` Photosensitive epilepsy risk in 3–60 Hz flicker band — hard gate.
- `[EMERGING]` "Subthreshold" flicker (>60 Hz) claimed non-provocative for PSE;
  literature supports this but individual susceptibility varies.

## Aspirational (kept for research; NOT wired into dose engine)

### 40 Hz gamma entrainment
- `[EMERGING]` Iaccarino/Tsai lineage: 40 Hz flicker → microglial response
  in mouse models. Human translation is active but unresolved.
- `[SPECULATIVE]` Claims of durable cognitive benefit in humans from
  consumer-grade 40 Hz devices. Kept as reading material; NOT a dose target.

### Broader visible spectrum claims
- `[SPECULATIVE]` Green (525 nm) analgesia for migraine — small trials,
  intriguing, non-load-bearing here.
- `[SPECULATIVE]` Blue-light circadian re-timing at specific lux×duration
  products beyond the well-established melanopic action spectrum.
- `[SPECULATIVE]` Any claim of "cellular resonance" at an unnamed frequency.
  Retained verbatim for the reader; not actionable.

### UV / short-wavelength
- `[PROVEN]` UV-B → vitamin D synthesis at documented MED fractions.
- `[SPECULATIVE]` Ultraviolet "biophoton" signalling in intact humans as a
  therapeutic lever. Kept; not wired.
