# Spec 04 — Commit Cadence

Prefer small merged PRs and frequent progress.

## Rules
- Merge often; avoid large long-lived PRs.
- No PR may remain open longer than 7 days without forced triage:
  - close, or
  - merge.
- Prefer many 1-file PRs over one 20-file PR.

## Exception
- Scaffold PRs (like WR PR #2) may include coordinated multi-file setup when required.
