## Executive Summary

The global photobiomodulation (PBM) therapy market is a high-growth medical device segment projected to expand from ~$281M in 2026 to ~$515M by 2033 at a ~10.6% CAGR ([Coherent Market Insights](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/industry-reports/photobiomodulation-market)). PBM uses low-level laser or LED light to stimulate cellular repair processes — applicable across pain management, wound healing, dermatology, and orthopedics.

The opportunity for Revvel: build a **market intelligence + treatment-tracking tool** that serves PBM device vendors, clinic operators, and practitioners. No dominant open-source or low-cost SaaS solution exists in this niche. A well-positioned tool can capture B2B SaaS revenue from the ~$281M+ ecosystem while also monetizing as a market research product (PDF report, Gumroad/Polar.sh).

**DECISION: PROCEED** — well-defined market with clear monetization vectors, strong CAGR signal, and no saturated OSS competition.

### Market Segmentation

**By Application (largest to smallest, estimated internal ranking):**
1. Pain Management — largest segment; chronic back pain and musculoskeletal treatments dominate clinical use
2. Wound Healing — strong hospital/SNF channel; reimbursement pathway improving post-CMS guidance
3. Dermatology — hair loss (LLLT helmets), acne, photorejuvenation; strong consumer/at-home sub-market
4. Orthopedics — sports medicine and post-surgical rehabilitation

**By Wavelength:**
- Near-infrared (NIR, 800-1100 nm) — deepest tissue penetration; preferred for pain/orthopedics
- Red light (600-700 nm) — skin surface treatments; dominant in consumer devices

**By End User:**
- Hospitals and specialty clinics (primary revenue)
- At-home consumer devices (fastest-growing unit volume, lower ASP)

## Risks

- **Pricing data gaps:** Three of seven identified competitors have no public pricing; competitive module will ship with "Pricing data pending" flags until resolved.
- **Market data freshness:** Coherent Market Insights data is 2026-2033 projection; actual figures will differ. Dashboard must display data vintage.
- **Consumer vs. clinical split:** At-home consumer devices (Joovv, PlatinumLED) follow different go-to-market than clinical devices (Erchonia, THOR). Product scope must commit to one primary persona to avoid dilution.
- **Regulatory sensitivity:** Any clinical claims in copy must be reviewed; PBM devices are FDA Class II medical devices — marketing language must stay within cleared indications.
