# WR: Smartphone-Driven Low-Power Light-Emitting Device - PMC

**Issue:** #15204

## Deliverable

Implemented a mobile-first red-light therapy planning tool at:
`projects/agent-generated/red-light-therapy-mobile/build/app/index.html`

## Included Capabilities

- Session duration calculator from dose and irradiance.
- Weekly schedule generator based on sessions per week and start day.
- Per-session safety warning for high planned dose.
- Educational use and clinical safety disclaimer.
