## Executive Summary
This WR documents the script variations and the necessary context for generating a LinkedIn avatar video using HeyGen. The video will promote the Green Software Foundation's Practitioner Certificate, aligning the creator's avatar and brand with eco-friendly architecture and cost-saving green coding principles.

## Step 1A — Product/Output Selections
- **Primary Output:** 3 Video Script Variations (Text)
- **Secondary Output:** LinkedIn post description and tags
- **Platform:** HeyGen for video generation, LinkedIn for distribution.

## Step 2 — Deep Web Research
### Market Insights
- **Target Audience:** Developers, DevOps engineers, and AI agent managers.
- **Hook Effectiveness:** Short, kinetic hooks are necessary for LinkedIn's fast-scrolling feed. Strategic hooks focusing on cost reduction and cloud infrastructure savings resonate highly with enterprise decision-makers.
- **Factual Accuracy:** The certification is officially hosted by the **Green Software Foundation** (founded under the Linux Foundation by entities like Microsoft and GitHub), not Linksys. This alignment increases credibility.

## Step 3 — Requirements
### Avatar & Video Constraints
- The avatar used must be saved and preserved for future projects.
- Visual settings must match the tone of each script (e.g., modern digital background, glassmorphic UI).

## Recommendations
- **A/B Testing:** Generate Option 1 and Option 3 to test which hook performs better on LinkedIn.
- **Avatar Consistency:** Ensure the HeyGen avatar's lighting and clothing match the professional tone of the scripts. Save the generated custom avatar in HeyGen for future reuse.

## Risks
- **Platform Changes:** LinkedIn's algorithm may penalize posts with external links. Including the link in the first comment rather than the description text could mitigate this risk.
- **Engagement Drop-off:** Avatar videos can sometimes feel unnatural if pacing and gestures are not fine-tuned. Ensure lip-sync and hand gestures in HeyGen are reviewed before final export.
