coldtrace.oaudrey.com

ColdTrace — temporal GIS for cold case investigations.

Pick a date range, draw an area of interest, and drop a last-known location pin. ColdTrace automatically fetches free LiDAR and satellite data, runs viewshed / line-of-sight analysis, detects temporal vegetation and terrain anomalies, and synthesizes a weighted probability heat map to prioritize search zones — for investigators, SAR coordinators, and forensic analysts. Built on WhiteboxTools, PDAL, GDAL, and Sentinel-2. 100% FOSS stack.

// 03 — Giving Pledge

Software with a
purpose.

A percentage of proceeds from every oAudrey product — software, mobile apps, autonomous systems, marketing and ecommerce — is donated to programs that support trafficking survivors with reskilling, recovery, and restoration.

This commitment is administered through Freedom Angel Fighters, the advocacy and anti-trafficking program of Freedom Angel Corp (Colorado, EIN 86-1209156, Non-Profit, Good Standing).

“End Trafficking and Violence of All Living Things In Mortal Danger of Extinction. Even A Spider In Sudan, Ooray.”

// Pledge breakdown
  • Reskilling
    Technical training, certifications, and mentor-matching.
  • Recovery
