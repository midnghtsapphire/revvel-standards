AI Hardware Selection Framework

Formal auditing and auto-correcting cost-benefit analysis for model training and inference pipelines. Evaluating CPU, GPU, TPU, and NPU architectures for cost-efficient self-managed MLOps.

Hardware Decision Tree

  • Are you training foundation models?
    • Yes → TPU (Cloud-scale, massive batch sizes)
    • No ↓
  • Do you need high-throughput batch inference or deep learning training?
    • Yes → GPU (HBM2/3, Tensor Cores)
    • No ↓
  • Is it a power-constrained edge application?
    • Yes → NPU (Real-time local inference, low power)
    • No ↓
  • Default: CPU (ETL processing, XGBoost, low-latency single request)
