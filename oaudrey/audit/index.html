How it works

  1. Intake. A 20-minute form and a scoping call. You tell us the sites, systems and constraints.
  2. Evidence. Two days of interviews, configuration review and read-only checks.
  3. Analysis. Each control is scored 0, 1 or 2. Missing evidence is marked unverified, never assumed.
  4. Report. Delivered on day five, with a one-page executive summary and the full findings register.
  5. Walkthrough. One call to turn the report into the first thirty days of work.

Questions

Do you need access to our systems?

Read-only access to the systems in scope, or screenshots and a shared session. No agents installed without agreement.

Is this a penetration test?

No. It is a configuration, exposure and readiness audit. Penetration testing is quoted separately.

How long does it take?

Five business days from access, assuming the intake form is returned within one day.

What if we have no security team?

That is the design. The report is written for one IT person and a leadership team that does not speak jargon.

Do you work with our MSP?

Yes. The audit can be delivered alongside your MSP, and the findings are written so they can act on them.

What happens after the report?

A 60-minute walkthrough and a 30/60/90 plan. Remediation support is optional and quoted separately.
