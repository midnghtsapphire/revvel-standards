# Fraud-Signal Verification Fleet
Measuring evidence, not manufacturing verdicts.

---
## The trap
"Detect this fraud" from one viral report =
confirming an accusation, not testing it.

---
## The reframe
We don't answer "is it fraud?"
We answer: **substantiated / unsubstantiated / unknowable** — per claim.

---
## The swarm
primary-source · financial · media-trace · **counter (red team)** · legal → **judge**

---
## Calibrated confidence
base = tier × provenance, + corroboration − contradiction,
**capped by tier AND by adjudication stage.**
Social post can never substantiate. Alleged can never read as convicted.

---
## Provenance: "how did they get it?"
document obtained > claim repeated.
"Exclusive"/"bombshell" = 0 evidentiary weight.

---
## Refusal doctrine
No fraud/guilt verdict on a named person. Ever. That belongs to courts.

---
## Live evidence ledger
Per-claim confidence meters, source trail, refusals in lockout state, auto-refresh.

---
## Seed case (Newsom DOJ-2026)
"He said DOJ is investigating" → WEAK (verified utterance, alleged matter).
"Newsom committed fraud" → **REFUSED**.
