## 1. Current-State Audit

- Does it still run?
- Are dependencies outdated?
- Are setup instructions still valid?
- Are docs usable by someone new?
- Are examples/screenshots outdated?
- Is distribution still working?
- Are credentials, endpoints, or integrations stale?

## 2. Market Recheck

- Does this category still matter?
- Has the market shifted?
- Did a better competitor appear?
- Has the problem become more urgent or less relevant?
- Are there new APIs, models, or agent frameworks that change the opportunity?

## 3. Product Relevance

- Is the core promise still valuable?
- Is the positioning clear?
- Is this product too broad or too vague?
- Would a narrower version be stronger?
- Is this now better suited as:
  - a product
  - a lead magnet
  - a client deliverable
  - an internal tool
  - an archive item

## 4. Missing Feature Analysis

- What critical features are now expected by the market?
- What features are missing for usability?
- What features are commodity and should NOT be prioritized?
- What is the one missing feature most likely to improve adoption?
- What integrations or packaging updates would make it more modern?

- Would repositioning change monetization potential?
