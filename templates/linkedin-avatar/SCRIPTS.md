## Usage Instructions

1. **Select Template** - Choose the script that best fits your brand voice and audience
2. **Customize** - Personalize with your own experiences and call-to-action
3. **Record** - Use HeyGen or similar platform to generate avatar video
4. **Optimize** - Add captions, music, and subtitles for maximum engagement

---

## Best Practices

- **Keep it under 60 seconds** for optimal LinkedIn engagement
- **Add captions** - 80% of users watch without sound
- **Include a CTA** - Link to your certification, portfolio, or content
- **Test multiple variations** - A/B test to find what resonates with your audience
