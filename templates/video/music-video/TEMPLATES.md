# Music Video Production Templates

Templates for creating music videos with HeyGen avatars and voice clones.

## Video Types

### Lyric Video
**Duration:** Song length (3-5 minutes typical)
**Best for:** Audio release, streaming platforms

### Narrative Video
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes
**Best for:** Storytelling, emotional connection

### Performance Video
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes
**Best for:** Artist showcase, music streaming

### Visualizer
**Duration:** Song length
**Best for:** Audio platforms, Spotify Canvas

---

## Production Workflow

```text
1. PRE-PRODUCTION
   ├── Song finalized and mastered
   ├── Lyrics approved
   ├── Concept developed
   ├── Storyboard created
   └── Avatar/voice selected

2. ASSET GENERATION
   ├── HeyGen video segments
   ├── Background visuals
   ├── B-roll footage
   └── Graphics/text overlays

3. POST-PRODUCTION
   ├── Audio sync
   ├── Color grading
   ├── Transitions
   └── Final export
```

## Template 1: Lyric Video

**Best for:** First release, maximum reach
**Duration:** Full song length

```text
[STRUCTURE]
[00:00-00:10] Title card with song name + artist
[00:10-INTRO] Visual intro with subtle lyrics
[VERSE 1] Lyrics with relevant imagery
[CHORUS] Bold, dynamic visuals
[VERSE 2] Different visual theme
[CHORUS] Repeat with variation
[BRIDGE] Atmospheric, emotional
[FINAL CHORUS] Highest energy
[OUTRO] Credits + streaming links

[AUDIO]
- Final mastered track
- Clean vocals for syncing (optional)

[VISUALS]
- Avatar singing/performing
- Abstract backgrounds
- Text animations for lyrics
- Brand logos (streaming links)
```

## Template 2: Narrative Video

**Best for:** Deep storytelling, emotional impact
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes

```text
[SCENE BREAKDOWN]

ACT 1 (0:00-1:00)
- Establish setting
- Introduce conflict/emotion
- Song intro plays

ACT 2 (1:00-3:00)
- Story develops
- Multiple scenes
- Avatar performs key moments

ACT 3 (3:00-4:00)
- Climax
- Resolution
- Final chorus

POST-CREDITS
- Streaming links
- Next release teaser
```

## Template 3: Performance Video

**Best for:** Artist branding, music platforms
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes

```text
[SETUP]
- Professional lighting
- Avatar in focal position
- Dynamic camera angles

[PERFORMANCE SECTIONS]
- Full performance shots
- Close-ups for emotional moments
- Wide shots for energy

[VISUAL EFFECTS]
- Subtle color grading
- Light effects synced to beat
- Background animations
```

## Music Video Specs

### Technical Requirements
- **Resolution:** 4K (3840x2160)
- **Frame Rate:** 24fps (cinematic) or 30fps
- **Format:** ProRes or H.264
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (YouTube), 9:16 (TikTok Reels)

### Platform Requirements

| Platform | Duration | Resolution | Format |
|----------|----------|------------|--------|
| YouTube | 3-5 min | 4K | MP4 |
| Spotify Canvas | 8-30 sec | 1080x1920 | MP4 |
| Apple Music | 3-5 min | 4K | MP4 |
| Instagram Reels | 15-60 sec | 1080x1920 | MP4 |
| TikTok | 15-60 sec | 1080x1920 | MP4 |

### Streaming Platform Checklist
- [ ] YouTube verified artist channel
- [ ] Spotify for Artists
- [ ] Apple Music for Artists
- [ ] VEVO account (if eligible)
- [ ] DistroKid or Tunecore account
