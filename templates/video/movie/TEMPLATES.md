# Movie Production Templates

Templates for creating full-length movies (90-120 minutes) using AI avatar technology.

## Movie Types

### Documentary
**Duration:** 60-90 minutes
**Best for:** Educational content, storytelling

### Short Film
**Duration:** 20-40 minutes
**Best for:** Festivals, streaming platforms

### Feature Film
**Duration:** 90-120 minutes
**Best for:** Streaming services, theatrical

---

## Production Workflow

```text
PHASE 1: DEVELOPMENT
├── Concept and logline
├── Treatment written
├── Script finalized
├── Budget approved
└── Avatar/voice casting

PHASE 2: PRE-PRODUCTION
├── Shot list created
├── Storyboard designed
├── Location/virtual sets determined
├── Schedule (segment recording)
└── Asset preparation

PHASE 3: PRODUCTION
├── HeyGen segment recording
├── B-roll capture
├── Voice recording
└── Reference footage

PHASE 4: POST-PRODUCTION
├── Edit assembly
├── Visual effects
├── Color grading
├── Sound design
└── Music scoring

PHASE 5: DELIVERY
├── Quality check
├── Format exports
├── Platform uploads
└── Marketing materials
```

## Template 1: Documentary Structure

**Best for:** Educational, biographical, topic exploration
**Duration:** 60-90 minutes

```text
[ACT 1: INTRODUCTION - 15 min]
├── Cold open (hook) - 2 min
├── Title sequence - 1 min
├── Introduce topic - 5 min
├── Establish stakes - 5 min
└── Promise of journey - 2 min

[ACT 2: EXPLORATION - 40 min]
├── Historical context - 10 min
├── Key figures/events - 15 min
├── Challenges faced - 10 min
├── Turning points - 5 min
└── Midpoint revelation - 0 min

[ACT 3: RESOLUTION - 20 min]
├── Current state - 5 min
├── Lessons learned - 5 min
├── Future implications - 5 min
├── Call to action - 3 min
└── Credits - 2 min
```

## Template 2: Short Film Structure

**Best for:** Tight narrative, emotional impact
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes

```text
[ACT 1: SETUP - 5 min]
├── Opening image - 1 min
├── Introduce protagonist - 2 min
├── Establish world - 1 min
└── Inciting incident - 1 min

[ACT 2: CONFRONTATION - 15 min]
├── Rising action - 5 min
├── Midpoint twist - 2 min
├── Complications - 5 min
└── Crisis point - 3 min

[ACT 3: RESOLUTION - 5 min]
├── Climax - 2 min
├── Falling action - 1 min
├── Final image - 1 min
└── Credits - 1 min
```

---

## Template 3: Feature Film Structure

**Best for:** Full narrative experience
**Duration:** 90-120 minutes

```text
[ACT 1: SETUP - 25 min]
├── Opening image - 2 min
├── Theme stated (subtle) - 1 min
├── Set up world - 5 min
├── Introduce protagonist - 5 min
├── Inciting incident - 3 min
├── Debate/checkpoint - 5 min
└── Break into Act 2 - 4 min

[ACT 2A: RESPONSE - 25 min]
├── New world/status quo - 5 min
├── Fun & games/excursions - 10 min
├── Midpoint - 5 min
└── Bad guys close in - 5 min

[ACT 2B: PRESSURE - 25 min]
├── All is lost moment - 5 min
├── Dark night of soul - 5 min
├── Insight/breakthrough - 5 min
└── Break into Act 3 - 10 min

[ACT 3: CONCLUSION - 20 min]
├── Final plan - 5 min
├── Executing plan - 5 min
├── Climax - 5 min
├── Final image - 3 min
└── Credits - 2 min
```

## Technical Specifications

### Movie Formats

| Standard | Resolution | Aspect Ratio | Frame Rate |
|----------|------------|--------------|------------|
| 4K UHD | 3840x2160 | 16:9 | 24/30fps |
| 1080p HD | 1920x1080 | 16:9 | 24fps |
| Cinema 2K | 2048x1080 | 17:9 | 24fps |
| IMAX | 4096x2160 | 1.90:1 | 24fps |

### Audio Specs
- **Dialogue:** -24 LUFS
- **Music:** -23 LUFS
- **Effects:** -22 LUFS
- **True Peak:** -1 dBTP
- **Channels:** 5.1 or Stereo

### Platform Delivery

| Platform | Format | Max Duration |
|----------|--------|--------------|
| Netflix | IMF/DCP | No limit |
| Amazon Prime | H.264/H.265 | No limit |
| Apple TV+ | ProRes/H.265 | No limit |
| Vimeo | H.264 | No limit |
| YouTube | H.264 | No limit |
