# Facebook Video Templates

Templates for Facebook videos, reels, and live streams.

## Video Types

### Reels (Short Form)
**Duration:** 15-60 seconds
**Best for:** Quick tips, engagement, viral reach

### Standard Posts
**Duration:** 1-3 minutes
**Best for:** Tutorials, testimonials, product demos

### Live Streams
**Duration:** 10-60 minutes
**Best for:** Q&A, product launches, behind-the-scenes

---

## Template 1: Quick Tip Reel

**Best for:** Viral reach, engagement
**Duration:** ~30 seconds

```text
[Visual/Setting] Casual, relatable environment. Avatar speaks directly to camera.

"Hey! Quick tip that will save you [time/money/resources]...

[Share the tip in 20-25 seconds]

Drop a 🔥 if this helped, and follow for more tips like this!"
```

---

## Template 2: Tutorial Standard

**Best for:** Value-driven content, authority building
**Duration:** 2-3 minutes

```text
[Visual/Setting] Professional setup with screen share or demo area.

"Welcome back! Today we're going to talk about [topic].

[Tutorial content in structured steps]

If you found this helpful, drop a comment below with your thoughts. And if you want more content like this, make sure to like and share!

Don't forget to subscribe and hit the notification bell so you never miss an update."
```

---

## Template 3: Testimonial/Social Proof

**Best for:** Trust building, conversions
**Duration:** 60-90 seconds

```text
[Visual/Setting] Clean, professional. May include testimonials overlay.

"Have you ever wondered what makes [ideal customer] so successful?

I asked [expert/person] about their secret, and here's what they said:

[Insert testimonial or quote]

If you want to learn more about how to [benefit], check out the link in my bio.

And let me know in the comments: what's your biggest challenge when it comes to [topic]?"
```

## Facebook Best Practices

### Video Specs
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical) for reels, 16:9 for standard
- **Duration:** 15 sec - 3 min (reels), up to 240 min (live)
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum
- **Format:** MP4

### Monetization
- Facebook In-Stream Ads (eligible at 10K followers)
- Branded content partnerships
- Affiliate links in posts
- Facebook Marketplace (for physical products)

### Engagement Tips
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Add captions (autogenerated or manual)
- Respond to comments within 1 hour
- Use trending audio when possible
- Cross-post to Instagram for maximum reach
