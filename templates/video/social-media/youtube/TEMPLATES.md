# YouTube Video Templates

Templates for YouTube videos with monetization in mind.

## Video Types

### Shorts
**Duration:** 15-60 seconds
**Monetization:** Shorts feed ads (when eligible)

### Standard Videos
**Duration:** 5-15 minutes
**Monetization:** Pre-roll, mid-roll ads, sponsorships

### Long-Form Content
**Duration:** 15-60 minutes
**Monetization:** Comprehensive ad revenue, course upsells

---

## Template 1: Tutorial/How-To

**Best for:** Evergreen content, search traffic
**Duration:** 8-12 minutes
**Revenue:** Ad revenue + potential affiliate

```text
[INTRO - 0:00-0:30]
[Hook + Intro]
"Welcome to [Channel Name]! In this video, I'm going to show you exactly how to [outcome].

By the end of this video, you'll know [specific skill or result]. Let's dive right in..."

[MAIN CONTENT - 0:30-10:00]
[Step-by-step breakdown with timestamps]
1. [Step 1] (2-3 min)
2. [Step 2] (3-4 min)
3. [Step 3] (2-3 min)

[OUTRO - 10:00-11:00]
[Summary + CTA]
"So there you have it! [Key takeaways]

If you found this helpful, smash that like button and subscribe for more content like this.

Drop a comment below with your thoughts, and I'll see you in the next video!"

[END SCREEN]
[Subscribe button, related videos, playlist]
```

## Template 2: List Video

**Best for:** High engagement, click-through rates
**Duration:** 10-15 minutes

```text
[INTRO]
"Today I'm sharing the [top X things/facts/reasons] about [topic] that [target audience] needs to know.

Let's get started!"

[MAIN CONTENT]
[Number each item with clear visual/title card]
1. [Item 1] - 2 min
2. [Item 2] - 2 min
...

[OUTRO]
"Those were my top [X] about [topic]. Which one surprised you most?

Let me know in the comments, and subscribe for more content like this!"
```

## Template 3: Explainer/ Educational

**Best for:** Authority building, evergreen traffic

## YouTube Best Practices

### Video Specs
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (standard), 9:16 (Shorts)
- **Duration:** 8+ minutes for optimal ad revenue
- **Resolution:** 4K preferred, 1080p minimum
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264)

### Monetization Streams
1. **YouTube Partner Program**
   - Pre-roll ads
   - Mid-roll ads (5+ min videos)
   - Shorts feed (when eligible)
   - Channel memberships

2. **Sponsorships**
   - Brand deals
   - Affiliate partnerships
   - Product reviews

3. **Cross-Platform**
   - Facebook monetized shares
   - TikTok revenue sharing
   - Patreon/Super Thanks

### SEO Optimization
- Keyword-rich titles (front-load)
- 200+ word description (first 2 lines visible)
- 15+ tags
- Custom thumbnail (1280x720)
- 5 cards, 2 end screens
