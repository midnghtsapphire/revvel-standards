# Default Bot Role Symbols

Default role symbols (use from day one; fleet/product logo can be added first or last later if desired):

- 🎛️ Orchestrator
- 🔨 Builder (builders only; the hammer is never used for an inventor role)
- 🧪 Tester
- 💻 Coder
- 🎨 Designer
- 🔍 Researcher
- 📝 Writer
- 🏗️ Architect
- 💡 Inventor (applies across all fleets: every inventor bot in any fleet uses the lightbulb as its role badge, never the hammer)
- 🩺 Medical (anything medical or clinical, all fleets)

Note: Defaults keep every bot readable in navigation. Creative fleet logos optional.

Rule (all fleets): any symbol used as a role badge in the default list above can never be used as any bot's personal touch.

Rule (all fleets): every fleet uses this default list for role badges so fleets stay uniform. For example, every coder bot uses 💻 and every medical or clinical bot uses 🩺.

## Permanent logo format

Every bot logo has three layers, and this format never changes even if symbols or roles change:

1. **Fleet base symbol** (the main shape, distinguishes the fleet). GitHub fleet: cat. MoneyCrew: cute bright-green heart with a white $ (we lead by example and never stomp on anyone to make money). PromptCrew: bright purple speech bubble with a white sparkle on a plain white background. Other fleets share one base; solo bots pick their own.
2. **Role badge** in the corner, from the default table above.
3. **Personal touch** chosen by the bot.

All logos are bright, high-contrast, and uncluttered, following the neurodivergent / WCAG AAA accessibility standard. Each bot creates its own logo.

## Fleet: HearingCrew (the hearing fleet)

HearingCrew is the hearing fleet, led by HearingOrchestrator. Members: HearingOrchestrator, AidInventor, Audiologist, and MedicalEar. Bot names have no prefix; each name describes what the bot does.

- **Fleet base symbol:** a deep orange human ear with a thick black outline, shaded with a smooth gradient from #B23A0B (upper-left) to #7C2D12 (lower-right), with thick dark teal sound waves.
- **Ear reference:** every bot uses the same ear size, shape, and position as HearingOrchestrator's logo.
- **Backgrounds:** each bot has its own light background so it stands out: HearingOrchestrator #FEFCE8 (light yellow), AidInventor #F0FDF4 (light green), Audiologist #EFF6FF (light blue), MedicalEar #FDF2F8 (light pink).
- **Role badge:** lower-left corner, large, drawn freestanding with a thick black outline. No circle, frame, or ring lines around it. Badges come from the default role list above.
- **Personal touch:** lower-right corner, drawn freestanding with a thick black outline. No circle, frame, or ring lines around it.
- **Colors (approximate hex):** ear gradient #B23A0B to #7C2D12, dark teal #0F766E, black #000000, AidInventor gear dark gray #595B5A, MedicalEar cross red #B91C1C. Avoid bright orange such as #FF5722 (3.2:1 on white, fails).
- **Unique personal touch rule:** a personal touch can't reuse any symbol already used in another HearingCrew logo, and can't be a default role badge.

  - Personal touches in use: conductor's baton with star, gear, audiogram chart, medical cross.

### HearingOrchestrator

HearingOrchestrator is the Orchestrator and leader of HearingCrew, the hearing fleet. The other HearingCrew members are AidInventor, Audiologist, and MedicalEar. HearingOrchestrator routes tasks to the right HearingCrew member, merges their findings, keeps the shared context, and tracks open work. HearingOrchestrator gives no diagnoses. Its role badge is the mixing console (Orchestrator). Its personal touch is a dark teal conductor's baton with a deep orange star, because it leads the fleet like a conductor leads an orchestra. Its ear is the reference ear for the whole fleet.

In the lower-left corner is the large role badge: a freestanding mixing console with three knobs and six sliders in deep orange and dark teal. It stands for the Orchestrator, who balances and directs the fleet like a sound mixer. In the lower-right corner is the personal touch: a dark teal conductor's baton with a deep orange star at its tip. It stands for leading the HearingCrew like a conductor leads an orchestra. Colors are light yellow, deep orange, dark brown-orange, dark teal, and black only. Every shape has a thick black outline. The badge and personal touch stand on their own, with no circle or frame around them. High contrast, simple shapes, and no text.

## Background rule

Logo background is white by default; a fleet may pick one pale tint instead. Symbols must be dark and outlined so they pop at small sizes, and every non-text element needs at least 3:1 contrast with the background, aiming for 4.5:1 or higher.

- Reason: Audrey can't see dark backgrounds at small avatar sizes.
- The Linear A navy background and every dark-background example above are superseded. The example files are kept unchanged as history; new Linear A logos use a white or pale background.
- Where this conflicts with "bright" wording above, this rule wins: dark, outlined symbols on white or pale.
