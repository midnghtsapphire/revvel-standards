# Archive resources

From the 2026-11-10 chat pastes. Not run. AI answers are leads, not sources.

The chats listed legal archive and open-access layers as the main collection path:

- Wayback Machine, including date-range and prefix operators, plus the CDX API
- Common Crawl, queried through a CDX index and, if needed, WARC extracts
- Memento Time Travel, for checking several archives at once
- Archive.today, for user-saved snapshots the Wayback Machine may miss
- Internet Archive Scholar, Unpaywall, CORE, OpenAlex, and BASE for open texts
- Europeana, Gallica, DPLA, and Chronicling America for scanned books and documents
- Zenodo, figshare, and OSF for preprints and datasets

Code sketches for Wayback CDX, Common Crawl CDX, Memento, and Arquivo.pt are under [`../code/ingest/`](../code/ingest/) and [`../code/src/`](../code/src/). They have not been executed.
