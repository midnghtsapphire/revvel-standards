# Soil eDNA Field Deployment Cheat Sheet & Lab Transmittal Protocol

## 1. Operational Overview & Field Sampling Purpose

Environmental DNA (eDNA) sampling provides Search and Rescue (SAR) teams and cold case investigators with a non-invasive, high-sensitivity method for detecting human biological presence in terrestrial and aquatic environments. When human remains or fluids interact with soil, cellular material leaches into the surrounding sediment matrix. However, environmental soil samples contain potent enzymatic inhibitors—primarily humic and fulvic acids, heavy metals, and tannic complexes—that co-extract with nucleic acids and block Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) amplification, leading to false negatives during Short Tandem Repeat (STR) genetic profiling.

This protocol provides field search leads and forensic evidence technicians with step-by-step instructions for:
1. Collecting sterile, contamination-free soil cores and sediment samples at high-probability target coordinates.
2. Identifying soil-specific PCR inhibition risks based on local pedology (e.g., alpine glacial till, alluvial clay, urban soot-silt).
3. Issuing clear, binding lab transmittal directives that mandate targeted chemical additives—specifically skim milk and Bovine Serum Albumin (BSA)—to neutralize humic binding and recover clean human STR profiles.

## 2. Field Sampling Protocol: Sterile Soil Core & Sediment Collection

Field operators must adhere strictly to aseptic sampling procedures to prevent cross-contamination from field personnel or surface biological activity.

### Field Equipment Checklist
* Stainless steel sediment core sampler (5 cm diameter x 30 cm length) or sterile Teflon core liners.
* Sterile disposable plastic spatulas and nitrided core caps.
* Nitrile gloves (powder-free; change between every individual sample core).
* DNA-Away / 10% bleach decontamination spray and isopropyl alcohol wipes.
* Sterile 50 mL polypropylene conical centrifuge tubes (RNase/DNase-free).
* Portable dry-ice cooler (-20°C maintaining) or liquid nitrogen vapor shipper for immediate field freeze.
* Waterproof indelible laboratory markers and tamper-evident custody seal tape.

### Step-by-Step Core Extraction Sequence

1. **Aseptic Site Preparation:** Clear surface organic leaf litter, loose debris, or fresh snow from a 15 cm x 15 cm sampling quad using a bleach-sterilized trowel. Do not scrape or disturb the top 1 cm of mineral soil.
2. **Core Insertion:** Vertical insertion of the sterile stainless steel core tube to a target depth of 15 cm to 30 cm into the subsurface sediment bed. In creek or drainage channels, target the low-velocity silt catchment layer beneath standing water.
3. **Core Extraction & Sectioning:** Carefully extract the core barrel. Wearing fresh nitrile gloves, section the core into 5 cm depth increments (0–5 cm, 5–10 cm, 10–15 cm, 15–20 cm). Place each 5 cm section into a dedicated sterile 50 mL conical tube.
4. **Immediate Stabilization:** Cap the tube, apply a tamper-evident seal, and immediately submerge the tube in the dry-ice cooler (-20°C) within 3 minutes of collection to halt microbial DNA degradation.
5. **Tool Decontamination:** Scrub core barrels with 10% bleach solution, rinse thoroughly with deionized water, and wipe with isopropyl alcohol before advancing to the next sample station.

## 4. Lab Extraction Protocol Directives by Soil Type

Forensic DNA laboratories receiving soil cores from these targets must apply specific pretreatment additives prior to DNA extraction and PCR master-mix preparation.

### A. Alpine Glacial Till & Volcanic Ash Soils (Target: `SHTUKA-2018-BC`)
* **Chemical Inhibition Profile:** Volcanic ash fractions (Andosol properties) and sub-alpine forest soils possess intense organic humic acid concentrations and strong mineral adsorption surfaces that bind nucleic acids and deactivate Taq polymerase.
* **Pretreatment Protocol:**
  1. Add **0.2 g skim milk powder per 1.0 g wet soil** during initial cell lysis buffering. Skim milk proteins competitively bind to reactive soil sites and humic compounds, releasing human DNA into solution.
  2. Supplement lysis buffer with **Bovine Serum Albumin (BSA) at a concentration of 10 mg/mL**.
  3. Conduct thermal incubation at **65°C for 30 minutes** with agitation to unbind adsorbed DNA fragments.
  4. Process supernatant through a Silica-Column (PowerSoil Pro) inhibitor-removal filter before STR thermocycling.

### B. Urban Silt, Soot & Hydrocarbon Matrices (Target: `SHAFFER-2006-OH`)
* **Chemical Inhibition Profile:** Urban subterranean vault sediments contain heavy loads of unburned hydrocarbons, motor oils, and fine carbon soot that pass through standard spin filters and inhibit PCR fluorescence during capillary electrophoresis.
* **Pretreatment Protocol:**
  1. Incorporate **BSA at 12 mg/mL** directly into the PCR amplification master mix to protect Taq polymerase from residual aromatic hydrocarbons.
  2. Perform dual-phase wash steps using 70% ethanol to strip volatile organic compounds before column elution.
  3. Utilize silica-membrane extraction kits optimized for low-yield, fragmented nuclear DNA recovery.

### C. Alluvial Silt-Clay & Drainage Sediments (Targets: `STEPPACH-2015-AR`, `TOMAN-2025-QC`, `LANDRY-2020-TX`)
* **Chemical Inhibition Profile:** Fine clay particles adsorb double-stranded DNA onto charged mineral surfaces, while alluvial humic acids co-elute with nucleic acids.
* **Pretreatment Protocol:**
  1. Perform high-speed mechanical bead-beating (garnet or zirconia beads) for 10 minutes to physically shear sediment-bound cellular matrices.
  2. Add **0.1 g skim milk per 1.0 g sediment** during cell lysis.
  3. Include **Proteinase K incubation (56°C for 45 minutes)** to digest soil enzymes and cellular proteins without damaging low-copy human DNA strands.

## 5. Cold-Chain Preservation & Lab Transmittal Form

All soil samples must be delivered to the forensic laboratory accompanied by a formal Transmittal Form and maintained under strict cold-chain custody.

### Shipping & Chain of Custody Guidelines
* **Cold-Chain Maintenance:** Samples must remain frozen at **-20°C or lower** during transit using dry ice (minimum 5 kg dry ice per 24 hours of shipping time). Never allow soil cores to thaw prior to chemical extraction, as freeze-thaw cycles accelerate DNA hydrolysis in acidic soils.
* **Tamper-Evident Packaging:** Each conical tube must be individually sealed in a heavy-duty biohazard bag with a unique serial-numbered security seal.
* **Laboratory Directives:** Attach the Transmittal Form directly to the outer shipping container and transmit a digital copy to the lead forensic DNA analyst prior to package arrival.
