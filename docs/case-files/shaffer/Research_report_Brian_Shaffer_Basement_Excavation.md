## Key Themes Across Sources

- **The 2020 Basement Excavation**: Columbus Police obtained a search warrant for a house near Lane Avenue after noticing fresh concrete in the basement. Excavation revealed an old fuel oil tank, but no body or forensic evidence of decomposition was found.
- **The Multi-Tipster Person of Interest**: Two separate tipsters in 2006 (including one who was with Brian that night) provided the same man's name. In 2026, a third independent tipster provided the identical name to podcaster Kelly Bruce.
- **Geospatial & Cellular Triangulation**: The excavated house sits near Lane Avenue, aligned with one of Brian's early cell phone tower ping sectors from April 1, 2006.
- **Confidence Assessment**: While investigative interest in the individual remains high, the physical confidence score that Brian's remains are located in that specific excavated basement is **0%** due to negative forensic excavation results.
