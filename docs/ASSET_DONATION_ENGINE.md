## FMV Calculation Methods

### For Products (New)

```text
FMV = Retail price at time of donation
    = What willing buyer would pay
    = Receipt from purchase
```

### For Products (Used)

```text
FMV = Fair Market Value
    = Thrift store value
    = Comparable sales
    = 20-50% of retail typically
```

### For Equipment

```text
FMV = Replacement cost - depreciation
    = Comparable sales
    = Professional appraisal (if >$5,000)
```

## Important Rules

### IRS Requirements for Charitable Deduction

1. **Donation must be to qualified organization**
   - LLCs can receive donations IF they're entities
   - Consult tax advisor for your structure

2. **Must have receipt**
   - Written acknowledgment from donee
   - Description of property
   - Good or service provided (usually none)

3. **FMV is at time of donation**
   - Not purchase price for used items
   - Document condition

4. **Donation must be complete**
   - Transfer ownership
   - Receipt signed
