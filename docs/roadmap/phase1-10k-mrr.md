- Create tiered sponsorship (Bronze $10, Silver $50, Gold $200, Platinum $1000)

- Pricing: Free (100 req/mo), Pro $29/mo (10k req), Business $199/mo (unlimited)
- Launch on Product Hunt, HN Show, and r/OSINT
- Target buyers: security researchers, journalists, compliance teams

### 3. Automated Product Pipeline ($2k/mo target)
- LLM-driven micro-SaaS generator (this repo's automation)
- Ship 1 micro-product per week via automated PRs
- Monetize via Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Polar checkout

- Fri: Metrics review + iteration

## KPIs
- MRR growth week-over-week
- Sponsor count
- API paid conversions
- Product Hunt / HN placements
