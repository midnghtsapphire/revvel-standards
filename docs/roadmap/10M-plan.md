- Sponsor tiers: $5, $25, $100, $500/mo

- Target: 200 sponsors + 50 products by Month 6

### 2. OSINT Tools

- One-off investigation reports ($500-$5000)

### 3. Automated Product Pipeline
- LLM-generated micro-SaaS
- Template marketplace
- Affiliate bundles

## Weekly Cadence
- Ship 1 product/week (Phase 1)
- Ship 3 products/week (Phase 2+)
- Automate onboarding, billing, delivery via Polar webhooks
