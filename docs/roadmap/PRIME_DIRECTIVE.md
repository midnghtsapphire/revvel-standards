# Prime Directive: $10k/month → $10M in 3 Years

## Mission
Scale from $10k/month recurring revenue to $10M total revenue within 36 months.

## Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Timeline | Target | Focus |
|-------|----------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Month 1-6 | $10k/month MRR | Polar.sh setup, OSINT MVP |
| 2 | Month 6-18 | $30k/month MRR | Product-market fit, automation |
| 3 | Month 18-30 | $100k/month MRR | Scale distribution, enterprise |
| 4 | Month 30-36 | $10M cumulative | Exit or lifestyle business |

## Revenue Pillars

### 1. Polar.sh (GitHub Funding)
- Sponsor tiers: $5, $25, $100, $500/mo
- Digital products: templates, tools, courses
- Recurring subscriptions on OSS extensions

### 2. OSINT Tools
- Self-serve SaaS: $29-$299/mo
- API credits model
- Enterprise contracts: $5k-$50k/year

### 3. Automated Product Pipeline
- AI-driven content → lead gen
- Auto-generated micro-tools
- Affiliate + info products

## Key Metrics (weekly review)
- MRR growth rate (target: 15%/mo phase 1-2)
- CAC payback (< 3 months)
- Churn (< 5% monthly)
- Free → paid conversion (> 2%)

## Non-Goals
- Vanity metrics without revenue impact
- Features without paying customers requesting them
- Fundraising before $30k MRR

## Weekly Cadence
1. **Mon** — Review metrics, set week priorities
2. **Tue-Thu** — Ship revenue-generating features
3. **Fri** — Distribution, marketing, outreach
4. **Sat** — Deep work / new product experiments
5. **Sun** — Rest, learn, plan
