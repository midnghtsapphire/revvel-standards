# Prime Directive: $10k/month → $10M in 3 Years

## Mission
Scale from **$10k/month** in month 1 to **$10M total revenue** by end of year 3.

## Phased Growth Plan

| Phase | Timeline | Target MRR | Cumulative Target |
|-------|----------|------------|-------------------|
| 1     | Month 1-6 | $10k/mo   | ~$60k             |
| 2     | Month 6-18 | $30k/mo  | ~$420k            |
| 3     | Month 18-30 | $100k/mo | ~$1.6M         |
| 4     | Month 30-36 | $300k+/mo | $10M total    |

## Focus Areas

### 1. Polar.sh — GitHub Funding Platform
- Sponsor tiers for OSS maintainers
- Automated tier upsell flows
- Digital product delivery (licenses, downloads)
- Recurring subscriptions with churn-reduction hooks

### 2. OSINT Tools
- SaaS OSINT dashboards
- API access (rate-limited free / paid tiers)
- Enterprise licenses
- White-label for security consultancies

### 3. Automated Product Pipeline
- Idea → prototype → launch in <14 days
- LLM-driven content, docs, and marketing
- CI-driven releases via GitHub Actions
- Analytics-driven pruning of underperformers

## Operating Principles
1. **Compounding over spikes** — recurring revenue > one-shots.
2. **Automate everything that repeats** — every manual step is a tax.
3. **Kill fast** — products missing milestones at 60 days get sunset.
4. **Distribution first** — build audience before build product where possible.
5. **Measure weekly** — MRR, churn, CAC, LTV, cash runway.

## Weekly Cadence
- Monday: Metrics review, prioritize
- Tue-Thu: Build / ship
- Friday: Launch, marketing push
- Sunday: Retro + next-week plan
