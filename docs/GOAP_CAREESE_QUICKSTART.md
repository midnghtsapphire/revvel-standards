# Goap Quick Guide for Careese

**One Page. Big Buttons. Simple Steps.**

---

## What Is This?

Goap is the robot helper that makes Reese-Reviews easy. You help Mom by clicking buttons. That's it!

---

## Your Main Jobs

### 1. Check the Dashboard (Every Monday)

🔗 **Link:** [Dashboard Link Here]

**Look for:**
- 📦 **Ordered** — Products coming soon
- 🚚 **Shipped** — Products on the way  
- ✅ **Arrived** — Products ready to review
- ⭐ **Vine** — Special Amazon products (most important!)

**What to do:**
- If "Arrived" has items → Click **"Start Review Process"** button
- That's it! The robot does the rest.

---

### 2. Upload Mom's Videos (When She Takes Them)

**Steps:**
1. Go to: [Video Upload Link]
2. Click **"Upload Video"** (big green button)
3. Choose Mom's video file
4. Click **"Process Video"**
5. Done! Robot makes 3 short clips + pictures automatically

---

### 3. Approve Review Drafts (When Robot Finishes)

**Steps:**
1. Check your email for: **"Review Draft Ready"**
2. Click link in email
3. Read the draft review
4. Click one button:
   - ✅ **"Looks Good!"** — Robot posts it
   - ✏️ **"Needs Changes"** — Tell robot what to fix
   - ❌ **"Skip This One"** — Robot saves it for later

---

### 4. Approve Resale Listings (For Stuff We Sell)

**Steps:**
1. Check email for: **"Listing Draft Ready"**
2. Click link
3. Look at the pictures and price
4. Click:
   - ✅ **"Post to Facebook"** 
   - ✅ **"Post to OfferUp"**
   - ✏️ **"Edit First"**

## Important Rules

1. **Don't stress!** If something looks wrong, just text Mom or skip it
2. **Big buttons only.** If you see code or confusing stuff, close it and tell Mom
3. **You can't break it.** The robot checks everything before posting
4. **It gets easier!** First week is learning, then it's 5 minutes a day

---

## Help! Something's Wrong

**If the dashboard won't load:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Still broken? Text Mom: "Dashboard down"

**If you can't upload a video:**
1. Check file size (must be under 2GB)
2. Check file type (must be .mp4 or .mov)
3. Still stuck? Text Mom: "Video upload stuck"

**If a draft looks weird:**
1. Click ❌ **"Skip This One"**
2. Text Mom: "Draft #[number] looks weird"
