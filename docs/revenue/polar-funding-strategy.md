# Polar.sh Funding Strategy → $10M in 3 Years

## Prime Directive
Start at $10k/month → Scale to $10M total by year 3.

## Phase Roadmap

| Phase | Timeline | MRR Target | Focus |
|-------|----------|------------|-------|
| 1 | Month 1-6 | $10k/mo | Polar.sh setup, OSINT MVP, first sponsors |
| 2 | Month 6-18 | $30k/mo | Tiered sponsorships, product launches |
| 3 | Month 18-30 | $100k/mo | Enterprise OSINT, automated pipeline |
| 4 | Month 30-36 | $10M total | Scale, acquisitions, exits |

## Phase 1: Foundation ($0 → $10k/month)

### Polar.sh Setup
- [ ] Create Polar.sh organization
- [ ] Connect GitHub sponsors
- [ ] Define sponsorship tiers:
  - $10/mo — Supporter (name in README)
  - $50/mo — Backer (logo in README)
  - $250/mo — Sponsor (logo + priority issues)
  - $1000/mo — Enterprise (dedicated support)
  - $5000/mo — Strategic (custom OSINT tooling)

### Revenue Targets
- 20 × $10 = $200
- 10 × $50 = $500
- 20 × $250 = $5,000
- 4 × $1000 = $4,000
- **Total: ~$9,700/mo → hits $10k target**

## Phase 2: Product Pipeline ($10k → $30k/month)

### OSINT Product Suite
1. **osint-cli** — Free CLI, drives funnel
2. **osint-pro** — $49/mo SaaS dashboard
3. **osint-enterprise** — $2k/mo custom deployments

- GitHub Actions → Polar.sh product sync
- Auto-invoice via Polar checkout links

## Phase 3: Scale ($30k → $100k/month)

- Hire (via revenue, not equity dilution)
- Enterprise contracts (annual pre-pay)
- Marketplace listings (AWS, GCP)

## Phase 4: $10M Total

- Sustained $100k/mo × 12 = $1.2M/yr recurring
- Enterprise deals: $50k-$500k contracts
- Potential acquisition offers

- CAC:LTV ratio (target: 1:3+)
- GitHub star → paying customer conversion
