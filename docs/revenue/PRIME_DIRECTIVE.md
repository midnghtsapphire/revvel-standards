# Prime Directive: $10k/month → $10M in 3 Years

## Mission
Scale from $10k MRR to $10M total revenue within 36 months via automated product pipeline, Polar.sh funding, and OSINT tools.

## Phase Milestones

| Phase | Timeline | Target MRR | Cumulative Revenue |
|-------|----------|------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Month 1–6 | $10k | $60k |
| 2 | Month 6–18 | $30k | $420k |
| 3 | Month 18–30 | $100k | $1.62M |
| 4 | Month 30–36 | $1M+ | $10M |

## Revenue Pillars

### 1. Polar.sh — GitHub Funding Platform
- Enable Polar.sh on all public repos
- Convert GitHub issues into paid bounties
- Offer sponsorship tiers ($5, $25, $100, $500/month)
- Sell digital products (templates, courses, tool licenses)
- **Target contribution:** 30% of total revenue

### 2. OSINT Tools
- Automated intel-gathering CLIs and SaaS
- Subscription API access ($29–$499/month)
- Enterprise licensing for security teams
- **Target contribution:** 40% of total revenue

### 3. Automated Product Pipeline
- Agent-driven code generation → shippable micro-products
- Publish, market, and monetize via Polar + Gumroad + Stripe
- Reinvest profit into infrastructure and paid acquisition
- **Target contribution:** 30% of total revenue

## Weekly Execution Loop
1. **Monday** — Ship 1 new product or feature
2. **Tuesday** — Publish 3 pieces of content (blog, X, LinkedIn)
3. **Wednesday** — Outreach: 20 prospects, 5 partnerships
4. **Thursday** — Optimize: pricing, funnel, retention metrics
5. **Friday** — Review revenue, kill losers, double winners

## Metrics That Matter
- **MRR growth rate** (target: 15%+ MoM in Phase 1–2)
- **Customer acquisition cost (CAC)** < 3× first-month revenue
- **Gross margin** > 80% (software-native products)
- **Churn** < 5% monthly

## Non-Negotiables
- Ship weekly. No exceptions.
- Every automated PR must move a revenue lever or reduce ops cost.
- Reinvest 40% of net profit into growth until $10M target hit.
