# Prime Directive: $10k/month → $10M in 3 Years

## Mission
Scale from $10k/month recurring revenue to $10M total revenue within 36 months.

## Phased Roadmap

| Phase | Timeframe | Target | Focus |
|-------|-----------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Month 1-6 | $10k/mo | Polar.sh funding, launch OSINT MVP |
| 2 | Month 6-18 | $30k/mo | Automated product pipeline, paid tiers |
| 3 | Month 18-30 | $100k/mo | Enterprise OSINT contracts, SaaS scaling |
| 4 | Month 30-36 | $10M total | Multi-product portfolio, acquisitions |

## Revenue Pillars

### 1. Polar.sh — GitHub Funding
- Enable sponsorship tiers on all public repos
- Convert stars/issues → paying supporters
- Target: $2k/mo baseline by Month 3

### 2. OSINT Tools
- Automated intelligence gathering products
- Freemium API + paid enterprise SLA
- Target: $5k/mo by Month 6, $50k/mo by Month 18

### 3. Automated Product Pipeline
- AI-driven code + product generation (OpenRouter → OpenHands fallback)
- Ship weekly micro-SaaS experiments
- Target: $3k/mo initially, compounding to $50k/mo

## Operating Principles
1. Free-tier first — zero infra cost until revenue justifies spend
2. Automate everything — issue → PR → merge → deploy
3. Ship daily — small increments compound
4. Measure MRR weekly — pivot on data, not opinion

## Fallback Chain
OpenRouter → OpenHands (opt-in) → manual review — all free-tier.
