## What it was used for

Agent framework behind a planned management SaaS (openclaw-ui), many skills, and agent onboarding docs.

## What worked

- Repos created: openclaw-skills-hub (2026-02-13), openclaw-ui (2026-02-25), OpenClaw_DigitalOcean (2026-02-17).
- Spec and blue-ocean research written 2026-02-25.

## What did not work

- Onboarding doc records an incident where 1,000 OpenClaw agents were compromised, as a lesson (docs/openclaw-agent-onboarding.md, third-party claim).

## Why kept or dropped

Unknown. Large spec and repo set exist; no dated decision to keep or stop.
